# 04 — Power and Thermal

## First rule

Every watt generated eventually becomes heat unless it is exported through work or radiation.

The Enterprise therefore has two coupled budgets:

- **power budget** — generation, conversion, storage, distribution, peak demand;
- **thermal budget** — internal dissipation, coolant transport, radiator area, radiator temperature, and failure tolerance.

## Baseline

A redundant nuclear-electric architecture is the near-term baseline for deep-space operations.

Fusion can replace the baseline only when its mass, reliability, shielding, maintenance, and thermal characteristics close at vehicle scale.

## Radiators are primary hardware

Radiator panels are not decoration and not optional. They are part of the propulsion and power system.

The architecture should make radiator replacement possible without exposing the crew to a single catastrophic maintenance operation.
