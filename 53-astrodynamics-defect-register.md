## 1. Executive Summary

This register formally documents astrodynamic defects, unphysical scalar velocity assumptions, vector directional mismatches, orbital geometry discrepancies, and model boundaries identified during Phase Astrodynamics & Digital-Twin Validation.

Previous project phases focused on **rocket-equation closure** (verifying that $12.250\text{ km/s}$ of vehicle $\Delta v$ is generated by burning $2,500\text{ t}$ of propellant). This audit tests **trajectory closure** (whether that $12.250\text{ km/s}$ can physically transport a $3,970.96\text{ t}$ spacecraft on an Earth-Mars-Earth round trip within 850 days under 2-body gravitational mechanics).

---

## 2. Categorization Framework

Every issue is classified by severity and analytical certainty:

### Severity Classification:
* **P0 — Mission-Invalidating:** Destroys mission feasibility regardless of secondary assumptions.
* **P1 — Trajectory-Invalidating:** Prevents trajectory closure on the stated schedule/geometry.
* **P2 — Significant Model Deficiency:** Omits key physical coupling or dynamic term.
* **P3 — Approximation/Documentation Issue:** Simplified model or terminology discrepancy.

### Analytical Certainty Class:
* **Proven:** Rigorously demonstrated by explicit first-principles vector calculation.
* **Assumed:** Accepted without solving governing orbital differential equations.
* **Approximate:** Represented with a reduced-order model.
* **Unresolved:** Cannot currently be established from repository physics.

## 4. Distinction Matrix: Proven vs Assumed vs Approximate vs Unresolved

### 1. PROVEN
* **Vehicle Rocket-Equation Velocity Capability:** Gross mass $3,970.96\text{ t}$, dry mass $1,470.96\text{ t}$, propellant $2,500.0\text{ t}$ yields **$12.250\text{ km/s}$ total velocity increment**.
* **Mass Conservation Law:** Enforced across all simulation events ($\Delta M = 0.0000\text{ t}$ error).
* **Radiator Thermal Rejection:** Stefan-Boltzmann double-sided emission at $850\text{ K}$ yields $133.35\text{ MW}_{th}$ rejection for $2,502.8\text{ m}^2$ composite array.
* **Low-Thrust Acceleration Magnitude:** $568.12\text{ N} / 2,581.73\text{ t} = 0.220\text{ mm/s}^2 = 22.43\text{ }\mu g$.

### 2. ASSUMED
* **Earth LEO Assembly Alignment:** Assumes 16 Super-Heavy launches deliver $3,970.96\text{ t}$ to LEO without orbital decay or orbital rendezvous failures over 6 months.
* **Reactor Continuous Operation:** Assumes $100\text{ MW}_{th}$ reactor operates for 850 days with zero unmonitored neutron poison transients or fuel element degradation.

### 3. APPROXIMATE
* **Heliocentric Transfer Geometry:** First-order patched-conic solution with coplanar circular orbits (Earth $1.00\text{ AU}$, Mars $1.524\text{ AU}$).
* **Crew Radiation Model:** Standard $0.07\text{ cSv/day}$ interplanetary GCR background and $52.25\text{ g/cm}^2$ storm shelter attenuation.

### 4. UNRESOLVED
* **180-Day Fast Return Propulsive Trajectory Closure:** Inbound transit requires $2.805\text{ km/s}$ more $\Delta v$ than available if zero-aerocapture propulsive Earth capture is enforced.
