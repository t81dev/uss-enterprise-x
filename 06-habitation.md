# 06 — Habitation

The crew compartment is an industrial habitat, not a hotel.

## Requirements

- closed-loop or high-recovery air and water systems;
- redundant atmosphere control;
- radiation refuge spaces;
- meaningful privacy and quiet;
- exercise and countermeasure systems for reduced gravity;
- medical isolation capability;
- maintainable hygiene systems;
- psychologically sustainable work/rest cycles.

## Artificial gravity

Long-duration missions should investigate rotating habitats or rotating sections where the system-level mass and complexity justify them.

A gravity system is only successful if it improves crew health without creating an even larger structural or operational problem.

## Architecture

Consumables, shielding, storage, and habitat should be spatially coupled where possible so useful mass performs multiple jobs.
