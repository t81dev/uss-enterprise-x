# 11 — Mission Profile

## Mission ladder

### 1. Orbital validation

Prove structure, propulsion, power, thermal rejection, autonomy, and rapid servicing.

### 2. Lunar / cislunar operations

Stress logistics and crew systems while rescue and return remain comparatively accessible.

### 3. Mars-class expedition

Validate years-scale autonomy, radiation management, closed-loop life support, and large-scale maintenance.

### 4. Uncrewed interstellar pathfinder

Send autonomous probes before risking humans.

### 5. Crewed interstellar expedition

Proceed only after the technology demonstrates a credible survival probability and recovery strategy.

## Mission doctrine

Do not go faster merely because the engine can. Go faster when the total mission system can absorb the consequences.
