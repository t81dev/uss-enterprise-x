# 14 — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Digital vehicle

Close preliminary mass, power, thermal, structural, radiation, and crew budgets.

## Phase 1 — Critical subsystem demonstrations

Demonstrate long-duration power, radiator behavior, autonomous maintenance, life support, and robotic servicing.

## Phase 2 — Orbital industrial demonstrator

Assemble and repeatedly service standardized modules in orbit.

## Phase 3 — Deep-space cargo vehicle

Prove autonomous navigation, communications-delay operations, radiation management, and long-duration propulsion without crew.

## Phase 4 — Crewed expedition vehicle

Validate the human systems in a smaller, recoverable configuration.

## Phase 5 — Enterprise X

Only after the architecture closes does the large Enterprise become a full engineering program.

## Parallel research branch — Occam-7 FTL

Investigate the physics of metric engineering as a separate research effort. It can rejoin the vehicle program only if the energy, stability, causality, and control problems become experimentally credible.
