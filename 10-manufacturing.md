# 10 — Manufacturing

The Enterprise cannot be economically designed as a bespoke artifact.

## Industrialization goals

- modular sections with standardized interfaces;
- high weld and inspection automation;
- digital production records;
- rapid nondestructive inspection;
- replaceable power, radiator, avionics, and life-support modules;
- common fasteners and connectors wherever practical;
- production tooling designed alongside the vehicle.

## Factory principle

A target such as one hull every few months is treated as a **manufacturing challenge**, not as a promise.

The factory rate must be derived from labor, tooling, inspection, supply chain, quality yield, test capacity, and launch cadence.

The cheapest vehicle is not necessarily the one with the cheapest raw material. It is the one with the least total recurring complexity.
