# 09 — Defensive Systems

The primary defense is not a weapon. It is the ability to **detect, avoid, absorb, isolate, repair, and continue operating**.

## Defensive stack

1. Early detection and trajectory prediction.
2. Maneuver and avoidance.
3. Layered physical debris protection.
4. Electronic and optical sensing redundancy.
5. Active interception against credible external threats.
6. Compartmentalization and rapid isolation after damage.

## Directed-energy branch

High-power lasers are considered for sensing, communications, and defensive interception. Thermal rejection remains the limiting system constraint.

## Kinetic-intercept branch

High-velocity kinetic interception is a speculative defensive architecture. The program studies energy, tracking, containment, and failure consequences at a system level without assuming a particular weapon implementation.

## Shield branch

A magnetic/plasma deflection field is retained as a frontier research concept for charged particles and radiation. It is not assumed to stop arbitrary macroscopic objects.
