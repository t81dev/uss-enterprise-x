# 07 — AI Autonomy

A deep-space ship cannot function as a remote-controlled appliance.

Communication delay turns autonomy into a structural requirement.

## Shipboard intelligence should provide

- navigation and fault isolation;
- predictive maintenance;
- resource forecasting;
- anomaly detection;
- life-support stabilization;
- robotic inspection and repair;
- mission scheduling;
- crew decision support.

## Design rule

The system does not need a fictional personality. It needs competence, observability, bounded authority, graceful degradation, and auditable decisions.

Human authority should remain explicit for high-consequence actions wherever practical.
