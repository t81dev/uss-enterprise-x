# 13 — Risk

## Catastrophic risk classes

- propulsion failure;
- power-loss cascade;
- thermal-system failure;
- pressure loss;
- radiation exposure;
- debris impact;
- fire or toxic contamination;
- software fault;
- autonomous decision error;
- crew incapacitation;
- inability to repair a critical subsystem.

## Rule

Every critical subsystem needs a degraded mode.

The design target is not “nothing ever breaks.” It is “one failure does not automatically become a mission-ending cascade.”

## Speculative-technology risk

Warp-like propulsion, advanced neural interfaces, artificial gravity, and field-based shields each receive independent technology gates. No speculative subsystem may become a single point of failure for the entire program until its evidence threshold is met.
