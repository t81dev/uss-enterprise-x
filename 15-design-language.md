# 15 — Design Language

The design should look like the result of constraint optimization.

## Expected visual character

- continuous primary hull;
- minimal external appendages;
- visible thermal architecture where useful;
- modular service zones;
- compact command volume;
- exposed structural logic rather than ornamental trim;
- replaceable panels and access paths;
- lighting used for operations, not decoration.

## What survives from Enterprise

The ship retains the ideas of:

- exploration;
- scientific independence;
- a multi-disciplinary crew;
- long-duration self-sufficiency;
- a recognizable command center;
- a vehicle that can become a home.

The silhouette is allowed to change.

The mission is not.
