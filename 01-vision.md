# 01 — Vision

## The reset

The original Enterprise is an extraordinarily effective narrative machine. As an engineering machine, however, it inherits assumptions from the era in which it was imagined.

Project Occam-7 resets the problem.

We are not trying to preserve the saucer because it is recognizable. We are not keeping engine nacelles because the silhouette demands them. We are not granting ourselves warp, inertial damping, force fields, transporters, or unlimited internal volume for free.

We begin with the mission.

## Mission statement

Build a reusable deep-space vehicle able to:

- carry humans safely for years;
- conduct science far from Earth;
- operate with long communication delays;
- repair and partially manufacture its own replacement hardware;
- survive radiation, vacuum, debris, thermal extremes, and subsystem failures;
- return and fly again rather than being consumed by a single expedition.

## Success criteria

The design is successful only when these close together:

**propulsion + power + thermal + structure + life support + autonomy + logistics + economics.**

A spectacular subsystem that breaks the system-level budget is not progress.
